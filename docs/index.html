YouTube Shorts workspace

Manage your Shorts workflow in one local studio.

ShortFlow Studio helps its owner connect YouTube channels, review channel analytics, organize content, schedule publishing, and upload videos from a private local workspace.

Designed for personal channel operations
  • Runs locally on the owner's computer
  • Uses Google OAuth for channel authorization
  • Keeps OAuth credentials and workspace data local
  • Does not sell Google user data

What ShortFlow does

A focused operating system for Shorts

The application requests only the Google permissions needed for the features the owner chooses to use. Read access powers channel dashboards and YouTube Analytics; upload access is requested separately when publishing is enabled.

Channel insights

View channel information, recent videos, performance metrics, audience geography, and estimated YouTube Partner Program progress.

Publishing workflow

Prepare metadata, remember channel-specific hashtags, upload a video, or schedule a private upload to become public later.

Local-first storage

Connected-account tokens, upload history, and workflow information remain on the computer running ShortFlow Studio.

ShortFlow Studio is an independent personal tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google or YouTube.
