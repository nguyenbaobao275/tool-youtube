ShortFlow Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how ShortFlow Studio accesses, uses, stores, protects, and deletes information when its owner connects a Google Account or YouTube channel. ShortFlow Studio is a personal, local-first application operated by its owner.

2. Information accessed

Depending on the permissions the owner grants, ShortFlow Studio may access:

  • YouTube channel identity, title, handle, thumbnail, subscriber count, view count, and video count.
  • Video metadata and statistics, including titles, thumbnails, publication status, views, likes, and related analytics.
  • YouTube Analytics reports such as engaged views, watch time, audience geography, and subscriber changes.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens required to maintain the authorized connection.
  • Video, audio, titles, descriptions, tags, scheduling details, and disclosure settings explicitly selected for upload by the owner.

3. How information is used

Google user data is used only to provide visible ShortFlow Studio features: displaying connected-channel dashboards, synchronizing analytics, showing recent or scheduled videos, uploading content requested by the owner, and scheduling publication requested by the owner. ShortFlow Studio does not use Google user data for advertising, credit decisions, surveillance, or unrelated profiling.

4. Local storage and transmission

OAuth tokens, channel connection records, workflow data, and upload history are stored locally on the computer running ShortFlow Studio, primarily in the application's .data directory or browser local storage. Information is transmitted to Google and YouTube only as required to perform an API request authorized by the owner. The developer does not operate a separate cloud database that receives this Google user data.

5. Sharing and sale

ShortFlow Studio does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. Data may be processed by Google and YouTube as necessary to provide their APIs and services, subject to their own policies.

6. Retention and deletion

Connection records remain on the local computer until the owner disconnects a channel or deletes the application's local data. Temporary upload files are removed after processing; abandoned temporary files are automatically cleaned after the configured retention period. Local upload history may remain until it is manually deleted. The owner can remove all locally stored application data by disconnecting channels and deleting the .data directory.

7. Access control and revocation

The owner can disconnect a channel from ShortFlow Studio at any time. Access can also be revoked from the Google Account permissions page. Revoking access prevents future API requests but does not automatically delete files already uploaded to YouTube; those can be managed in YouTube Studio.

8. Security

The local backend listens on the loopback interface and applies local-origin and request validation controls. OAuth client secrets and tokens must not be committed to source control or shared. No security method is guaranteed to eliminate every risk, so the owner is responsible for securing the computer and Google Account used with the application.

9. Google API Services

ShortFlow Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube features is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when application functionality or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to nguyenbaobao7575@gmail.com.
